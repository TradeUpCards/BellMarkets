6. Trade path 3: Buy NO (atomic mint_pair + sell-YES)

Step 6.1 — Buy NO Market, 10 contracts CORE DEMO + ATOMIC

Setup
  • Action: Buy
  • Outcome: NO
  • Order type: Market (Limit should be DISABLED with tooltip "Limit Buy NO coming in v1.5")
  • Amount: 10
What happens on-chain
  • mint_pair(10) — pays 10 × $1 bUSDC + 2% fee to vault, gets 10 YES + 10 NO
  • Phoenix-style atomic Sell-YES → sells 10 YES at best bid ($0.50) → receives $5
  • Net: spent ~$10.20 (mint + fee) − $5.00 (YES sale) = ~$5.20 for 10 NO
PASS: tx succeeds with ONE signature, ONE Phantom popup. Position: YES: 0 / NO: 10. bUSDC down ~$5.20. The atomic sequencing means user never sees an intermediate "YES: 10" state.
FAIL #1: tx fails with BuyNoIocError → bid book too thin OR IOC defense fired (book can't absorb 10 contracts at acceptable slippage). Try smaller amount (3 contracts).
FAIL #2: tx succeeds but user ends with YES: 10 AND NO: 10 → IOC defense BYPASSED. This is a Cleo build-buy-no.ts regression. STOP THE DEMO and flag to Cleo immediately.

11. Settle + redeem (post-settle)

Step 11.1 — Settle a strike (requires post-expiry OR admin override)

The demo strikes are seeded with expiry 2026-05-27 — NOT yet expired today (2026-05-25). To demo settle:

  • Option A — Skip live settle in the demo. Narrate the flow + show on-chain settle txs from prior Day-4 / Day-5 META markets that have already settled.
  • Option B — Use admin_settle via the admin dashboard (if Cleo ships P5) or via CLI: pnpm admin-settle --strike <PUBKEY> --outcome Yes. NOTE: admin_settle is gated by admin_override_eligible_at — default 30 days post-expiry per DR-008. May not be callable yet on the seeded strikes.
  • Option C — Create a fresh strike with near-future expiry via user_create_strike_market set to expire ~5 min from now. Trade against it. Then settle after expiry.

Option A is safest for demo. Options B/C require setup time.

Step 11.2 — Once settled, click Redeem on winning side

Per market, after settle, the winning side's "Redeem" button should activate. Click → burns winning tokens → vault releases $1 per token.
