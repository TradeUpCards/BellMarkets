How it works

non-custodial · 4 steps
01 / pick

Pick a market

Choose a MAG7 stock and a strike. "Will META close above $680 today?"

02 / trade

Trade Yes or No

Buy Yes if bullish, Buy No if bearish. Sell anytime before 4 PM ET. One signature per action.

03 / settle

Settle on-chain

At 4 PM ET, Pyth oracle posts the closing price. Outcome writes immutably to chain.

04 / redeem

Redeem USDC

Winning tokens claim $1 USDC each. Burn losing tokens. Redeemable indefinitely.
